A proposed identity for Veloce Driving. The firm's value is judgement, knowing what a driver, a racing family or a car owner should do next, so the brand asks one question: where next?

Everything here is a first proposal for discussion: the wordmark, colours, lettering, the parts the brand is built from, how it speaks and a few places it would live.

How it speaks.

  • Asks, then answers.“Where next?” followed by something specific: the next lap, the next season, the next car.
  • Plain and exact.“Bespoke days at UTAC Millbrook from £1,500.” Facts before adjectives.
  • Personal.“You deal with Kat or Richard.” Names, not “our team”.
  • Independent.“No commissions from dealers or brokers.” Said once, clearly.
  • Never.Supercar-experience hype, “luxury lifestyle”, or anything that makes motorsport sound closed to newcomers.

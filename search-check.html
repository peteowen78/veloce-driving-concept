Your site passes 8 of the 10 basic checks. That is a strong result. The site is well set up for search, with two gaps worth closing.

The one that matters most

Shared links carry no picture.

When someone shares your site in a message, on LinkedIn or on social media, the title and description appear but no image goes with them.

The site is set up to show a large picture on shares, but no picture has been chosen. For a motorsport business, where the cars and the circuits do much of the persuading, that is a missed opportunity every time the link is passed on.

One smaller thing to fix

This one is quick to put right.

To fix

The home page has no main heading

Search engines read a page's main heading to work out what it is about. Your home page does not have one. The first headings they meet are "Welcome To", which says nothing about motorsport management or driver development.

A new site would open with a heading that says what Veloce does, in the words a client would search for.

To fix

Your phone number and address are written out

Neither is written on your home page. Your fact sheet for Google carries them, which helps, but an AI tool reading the page itself will not find them.

To fix

A summary file for AI tools

There is none. It is a newer idea, a short plain-text summary of what Veloce does and who for, and it takes about twenty minutes to write.

Your site is otherwise well placed here: it has a great deal of readable text and a fact sheet already.

Nobody can make an AI recommend a business, and anyone who offers to is selling nothing. What a website can do is let these tools reach it, read it and get your facts right.
